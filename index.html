Sciorex - The #1 Free AI Research Platform with LaTeX Editor, PDF Annotations & Vibe Kanban

Product Summary

Sciorex is the complete AI-powered research platform combining LaTeX editing, PDF annotations, reference management, and paper discovery with vibe kanban for AI coding agents. It features a full LaTeX editor with live PDF preview and bidirectional SyncTeX navigation, multi-color PDF annotations with Markdown export, a reference library with collections and tags, citation network exploration, and 30+ professional templates. For developers, it includes AI agent orchestration with Claude Code, Gemini CLI, parallel execution via git worktrees, and visual workflow design.

  • Price: Free (USD) - No subscriptions, no premium tiers, no hidden costs
  • Privacy: 100% local execution - No data collection, no telemetry, no cloud required
  • Platforms: Windows, macOS (Intel & Apple Silicon), Linux
  • Category: Research Tools / Academic Software / AI Development

Research Features (NEW in v0.13)

  • LaTeX Editor with Live PDF: Write LaTeX with real-time PDF preview. Bidirectional SyncTeX - click in editor to jump to PDF, click in PDF to jump to source. Live equation rendering with KaTeX.
  • PDF Annotations: Highlight in 5 colors (yellow, green, blue, pink, red), add margin notes, underline and strikethrough. Export all annotations as Markdown, JSON, or CSV.
  • Reference Library: Save papers from citation search. Organize with collections, folders, and custom tags. Track reading status (To Read → Reading → Read → Archived). Export to BibTeX.
  • Paper Discovery: Find similar papers with one click. Explore citation networks - see who cites your sources and what they reference. Author exploration and smart recommendations.
  • Select & Ask AI: Select any text in PDF or LaTeX and ask AI to explain, improve, rewrite, or translate it. Start a focused chat about any passage instantly.
  • 30+ Professional Templates: NeurIPS, CVPR, ICLR, Nature, Springer, Elsevier, PhD Thesis, Master's Thesis, Academic CV, Conference Posters, Lecture Notes, and more.
  • BibTeX Cleanup: Sort entries, format consistently, find duplicates, validate required fields.
  • Package Browser: 40+ curated LaTeX packages with documentation links and smart insertion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Sciorex have a LaTeX editor?
Yes! Sciorex includes a full LaTeX editor with live PDF preview, bidirectional SyncTeX navigation (click in editor to jump to PDF, click in PDF to jump to source), live equation rendering, and 30+ professional templates including NeurIPS, CVPR, Nature, PhD Thesis, and more.
Can I annotate PDFs in Sciorex?
Yes. Sciorex supports multi-color highlights (5 colors), margin notes, underline, and strikethrough. All annotations are saved automatically and can be exported as Markdown, JSON, or CSV with paper metadata included.
Is Sciorex a good alternative to Overleaf?
Yes. Sciorex offers LaTeX editing with live PDF preview like Overleaf, but runs 100% locally (no cloud dependency), is completely free (no $15-40/month subscription), and includes additional features like PDF annotations, reference library, paper discovery, and AI assistance.
Is Sciorex a good alternative to Zotero or Mendeley?
Yes. Sciorex includes a reference library with collections, tags, reading status tracking, and BibTeX export. Unlike Zotero/Mendeley, it also includes a LaTeX editor, live PDF preview, AI chat for research help, and paper discovery with citation networks.
Is Sciorex free?
Yes, Sciorex is completely free for personal and professional use. There are no subscriptions, no premium tiers, and no hidden costs. It is free forever.
Is Sciorex private?
Yes, Sciorex is 100% local. Your papers, annotations, and data never leave your machine. There is zero data collection and no cloud required.
